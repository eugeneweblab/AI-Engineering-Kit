# Checklists

## Purpose

Cross-cutting checklists for moments that span several topics at once — launching a site,
opening a pull request, starting a project. Each item is a yes/no question with a verifiable
answer.

Topic-specific checklists live with their topic as `98-production-checklist`,
`99-ai-review-checklist`, and `100-common-antipatterns`. These are the ones that do not
belong to any single topic because they touch all of them.

## How to Use a Checklist

- **Answer, do not skim.** An item you cannot verify is unchecked, not assumed.
- **Unchecked is a decision, not a failure.** Some items will not apply. Say why rather than
  quietly ticking them.
- **Run it at the stated moment.** A pre-launch checklist run the morning of launch finds
  problems there is no time to fix.
- **Delete what nobody checks.** A list people tick blindly is worse than a shorter list they
  actually run.
